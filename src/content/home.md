## What we do

A club for students who like creating things, solving problems, and learning
useful skills. It's **free to join**, and no experience is needed.

- **CS lessons** for anyone learning to code
- **Member socials** to meet people who like computers
- **Competitions**, from hackathons around the world to UIL
- **Sub-clubs** and other CS-related opportunities

## Why join?

**New to CS?** Learn skills that are useful in almost every career, build
projects, and make friends.

**Experienced?** Compete around the world, earn service hours, and solve real
problems.

## Why learn to code?

- **Tackle real-world problems.** Solve problems in your community with
  research projects, inventions, and more.
- **Computational thinking.** Coding teaches problem solving that works in any
  field: debugging, finding efficient solutions, and abstraction.
- **Career applications.** Coding is useful well beyond STEM, in business,
  finance, medicine, and more.

## Competitions

### Hackathons

We find hackathons all over the world, both in person and virtual. You build an
app or project, present it to judges, and compete for awards. The focus is on
creating projects.

### UIL

Theory-based competitions aligned with the AP curriculum, with both individual
and team components. The focus is on test taking.

## Sub-clubs

### CSHS

The CS honor society. Earn a graduation cord, strengthen your college
application, help organize competitions, and take part in outreach programs.

To join, you need:

- At least 1 completed CS credit
- A 3.7+ weighted GPA
- To be in 10th grade or higher
- To complete the required service hours during the year (details coming soon)

### Girls Who Code

Girls Who Code is an international nonprofit working to close the gender gap in
technology. Our chapter introduces CRJH students to coding and its career
applications. Meetings are at the CRJH Library on Tuesdays, 4-5 PM.
